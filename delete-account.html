Delete your Smart Budget Manager account

Smart Budget Manager allows users to request deletion of their account and associated app data.

Delete from the app

  1. Sign in to Smart Budget Manager.
  2. Open Profile & Settings.
  3. Open Data & Privacy.
  4. Select Delete account & data.
  5. Type DELETE when prompted and confirm permanent deletion.

If you cannot access the app

Email krishna.vijay04@gmail.com from the email address registered to your Smart Budget Manager account. Use the subject Delete my Smart Budget Manager account.

Please include enough information for us to identify the account you want deleted. Do not send your password.

What is deleted

The deletion process removes the authentication account and associated app records, including profile information, accounts, cards, transactions, loans, investments, education records, attendance records, categories, and related app data from the active application database.

Important

Account deletion is permanent. Export any records you want to keep before requesting deletion. Infrastructure providers may retain limited backup or security records for a short period in accordance with their operational and legal requirements.
