Smart Budget Manager Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

Smart Budget Manager is a personal finance and expense management app. It processes information you provide to authenticate your account, store your records, synchronize your data, and provide the features you choose to use.

Information processed

Account information may include your display name, email address, and account identifier. User-entered financial information may include accounts, balances, transactions, card details such as the last four digits and limits, loans, repayments, investments, and related notes. If you use Education or Attendance features, records you enter for those features may also be stored.

How information is used

Information is used to provide app functionality, including sign-in, synchronization, calculations, reports, backup/export features, and restoring your data when you sign in on a supported device.

Cloud storage and service providers

Smart Budget Manager uses Supabase for authentication and cloud database services. Data entered into the app is sent to and stored using this service so the app can provide its core features.

Sharing

Smart Budget Manager does not sell personal or financial information and does not use it for advertising. The app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs. When you use an export or share feature, information is shared only after you initiate that action and choose a destination using your device sharing options.

Security

The app uses secure network connections when communicating with backend services. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Data retention and deletion

Your app data is retained while your account remains active or as needed to provide the service. In the app, go to Profile & Settings > Data & Privacy > Delete account & data to permanently delete your account and associated Smart Budget Manager records from the active application database.

If you cannot access the app, you can request account deletion by emailing krishna.vijay04@gmail.com from the email address registered to your Smart Budget Manager account.

Infrastructure providers may retain limited backup or security records for a short period in accordance with their operational and legal requirements.

Your choices

You control the records you enter. You can edit or delete individual records where the app provides those controls, export supported records, or delete your entire account and associated data.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion assistance, contact krishna.vijay04@gmail.com.
